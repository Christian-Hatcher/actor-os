What's locked in v5

  • Aesthetic — warm near-black canvas, paper-white type, Instrument Serif italic for the briefing voice. The whole product reads like a film title card crossed with a call sheet.
  • Hierarchy — Greeting → prose briefing (CTA: Open day) → week strip → collapsed section accordion (Auditions · Earnings · Self-tapes · Contracts · Outreach · Inbox).
  • Color codes — green = banked / done. amber = today / callback. red = urgent (overdue tape, contract flags). blue = submitted. purple = personal.
  • Bolded names in the briefing get a faint amber underline highlight so they survive a glance.
  • Section meta numbers flip color when the row is urgent.

What I'd touch next

  • Hi-fi the audition detail page — same vocabulary, the call-sheet block lit by the poster image.
  • Hi-fi the splash as a 1.4s motion sketch (animations starter).
  • Onboarding (photo upload, Gmail, Calendar MCP, currency).

Caveats

  • Avatar shows initials; user photo lands at integration.
  • Chart values are illustrative — wire to the existing Supabase data layer.
  • Accordion state is in-memory; persist to localStorage when wiring.
