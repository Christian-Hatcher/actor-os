Splash — Cold open

2.5s splash · portrait · ivory locked
When it plays
First cold-open of the day only. Subsequent opens skip directly to the dashboard. State persists in localStorage under last_splash_date. Tap anywhere mid-sequence to skip.
Sequence
Black → letterbox bars in → photo fades in behind bars → slate metadata types in → AO mark pops in bottom-left letterspaced wide → tight → hold → cross-fade to briefing.
Audio (optional)
Defaults off. Faint film-reel click on bar reveal, low sustained tone under photo, click-clack as slate metadata types in. Settings can re-enable.

Decisions in this motion

  • Letterbox bars draw in vertically (not horizontally) — feels like a curtain rising.
  • Photo arrives with a slight 1.04 → 1.0 scale-down so it lands rather than zooms.
  • Slate metadata types char-by-char in mono, like a real digital slate.
  • AO mark letterspaced wide → tight is the signature gesture — wide letterspacing reads "title card," tight reads "logo." Going from one to the other is the brand moment.
  • Cross-fade to briefing at 2.0s — the splash hands you off to "Good morning, Christian."
  • No audio by default — actors open the app on set, in lobbies, on trains. Silence is the right default.

Caveats

  • Photo block is a hatched placeholder — fed by the actor's onboarded headshot / on-set still / stage still.
  • Slate metadata is illustrative; production build pulls real date, project, and an "ACTOROS" mark.
  • Animation duration loops at 3.5s for inspection. Production duration is 1.4s + 0.6s cross-fade.
