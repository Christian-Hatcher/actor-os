Recording rules

What's on screen
  • Sides drawer at the bottom — script visible while you act. Drag down to dismiss.
  • Audio meter — turns red on clip.
  • Rule-of-thirds grid always on (light).
  • Camera flip for solo work.
  • Timer top-right, against the configured max runtime.
When to stop
  • Long-press the big red button to end. (Prevents accidental stops.)
  • Tap once = bookmark a beat — surfaces in trim editor.
  • Auto-stop at runtime cap with a 3-second warning.
After stop
  1. Take appears in the takes carousel.
  2. Returns to tape detail page with new take selected.
  3. If hit take limit, prompt to delete oldest take before re-recording.

  • List view — three card states (empty / draft / submitted), constraints surfaced (partner, take cap, runtime).
  • Tape detail — portrait player + takes carousel + audition-style metadata + submit flow.
  • Recording UI — sides drawer (script visible), audio meter, rule-of-thirds, long-press to stop.
  • Submitted with feedback — CD's reply parsed inline, callback ✓ outcome, actor's private notes.
  • Desktop — 3-column grid using the same card.

Locked decisions

  • Tapes belong to auditions; CD/Constraints/Partner are pulled, not retyped.
  • Submit emails the CD, logs Outreach, syncs to cloud, updates audition status — one tap.
  • CD's reply gets parsed into the feedback card automatically; manual fallback for forwarded texts.
  • Actor's notes are private, timecode-attachable, and searchable across all tapes.
