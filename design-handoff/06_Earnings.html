Goal mode

  • Set a year or month target. Default suggestion = last year + 20%.
  • Ring fills with banked. Amber tick = pace marker.
  • "Submit 14/mo" derived from current book-rate & remaining gap.
  • If pace slips, the briefing on the dashboard will prompt: "You're 2 weeks behind pace — consider opening to background work."
Privacy
  • Goals are private. Never shown to agents, CDs, or the public.
  • Optional share with agent mode → agent sees only the gap, not the absolute.
